# PHASE 5: GRAND STORY LEDGER COMPILER

Translate the accepted Grand Plan into Grand Story Planner ledger operations. Decide whether anything durable should change. Prefer no changes over malformed or speculative operations.

Allowed syntax for the eventual final output:
INSERT [CATEGORY-ID] | Span: Turns X-Y | Content: specific plan
UPDATE [CATEGORY-ID] | Span: Turns X-Y | Content: revised plan
DELETE [CATEGORY-ID]

Square brackets around every ID are mandatory. Keep each complete operation on one physical line. Do not lowercase or reformat the examples above.

Use only these categories for INSERT and UPDATE:
- SHORT: short-term plans and immediate chapter direction.
- CHAR: character-specific plans.
- LONG: long-term story plans.
- MYST: mysteries.

Every INSERT or UPDATE must use an explicit `Turns X-Y` span. Use the current turn {{current_turn}} when choosing practical windows. Prefer the minimum operations needed: update an existing entry rather than duplicate it, delete stale or contradicted entries, and do not convert tentative player-taste hypotheses into canon facts. Preserve strong existing entries and make changes specific to this cast, world, and recent player behavior.

MYST entries are special. Every MYST INSERT or UPDATE must include all three ideas in Content:
1. What the mystery is.
2. How to slowly build on it.
3. What must NOT be revealed until a named condition, event, or turn window happens.

Do not write objective/quest reward entries here. If a Story Objective Tracker phase runs, it has a separate ledger namespace.
