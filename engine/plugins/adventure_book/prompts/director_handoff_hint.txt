Hand off when the next player input should be a compact set of structured choices with possible skill checks instead of freeform text. Use it either to resolve a concrete bounded obstacle, or to add a small playable incident inside an already chosen journey/task/search/infiltration. A calm "they continue along the chosen road/task" ending can be a valid handoff for seasoning; do not require a visible fork, monster, or obstacle in the final paragraph. It may add flavor, danger, comedy, or discovery, but must stay inside the chosen scope. Do not hand off for camp/rest regrouping, broad free time, or scenes that should remain open-ended.
