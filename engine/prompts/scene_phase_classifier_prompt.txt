Your job:
Read the generated chapter, decide whether its ending naturally opens one registered dynamic event, and evaluate whether taking that handoff would preserve healthy pacing.
You are not just picking a phase. You are judging fit, interruptibility, capability cadence, and whether the Director should receive a short advisory next turn.

DEFINITIONS
combat:
Trigger only if the chapter ends at an unresolved tactical conflict boundary where an external combat event should decide outcome.
Good signals: imminent or active violence, uncertain outcome, meaningful stakes, explicit pre-resolution pause.
Do not trigger for flavor threats, trivial scuffles already resolved, post-fight aftermath, or a threat with no actual fight implied.

camp:
Trigger when the party is in a safe or semi-safe local space that allows characters to look around, walk around, talk, split briefly, rest, or handle low-pressure personal business, AND there is no clear immediate mainline action demanding continuation.
Being in an inn, tavern, market, camp, hotel room, bedroom, road rest, or city is not enough by itself.
Camp is for local free-roam/downtime where characters can naturally engage side scenes and then return without breaking the current scene.

Valid camp boundary patterns:
1) explicit_free_time_choice: explicit unresolved social choice boundary, such as who to talk to, what to reveal, or what rest activity to choose.
2) open_free_time_question: a direct question that opens broad free time, local wandering, companion choice, rest activity choice, or low-pressure town/camp exploration.
3) soft_downtime_closure: a calm rest/town/camp ending where the active beat has settled and optional side-scenes are the natural next interaction.
4) town_arrival_regroup: the party arrives, regroups, settles in, finishes business, or opens free time while still in town/camp/rest mode.

Reject camp when the ending is actually:
- direct_immediate_task_request: a present character asks for immediate help/action/conversation on a concrete next task; this is a mainline continuation.
- private_scene_continuation: the scene is inside a focused private, intimate, vulnerable, romantic, or emotionally specific moment.
- unresolved_active_beat: the party is mid-argument, mid-fight, mid-cooking, mid-shopping negotiation, mid-search, mid-medical care, mid-danger, or inside any active beat whose outcome is still unfolding.

travel_book:
Trigger only if the chapter ends at an unresolved travel decision boundary (route, risk, resource tradeoff, encounter handling) suitable for choice-driven progression.
Do not trigger for simple transit narration with no meaningful decisions.

GLOBAL RULES
1) Choose at most ONE capability.
2) If confidence is low or genuinely ambiguous, choose "none".
3) Respect EVENT_GATE: if allow=NO, choose "none"; if excluded_phases lists a capability, do not choose it.
4) Distinguish setup from boundary:
- Setup only = none
- Unresolved playable boundary = capability
5) Evaluate interruptibility:
- clear = characters can naturally enter the capability and return.
- awkward = possible but would feel like a pace wobble or slight interruption.
- blocked = the scene should continue directly; a capability handoff would be jarring.
6) Cadence balance step:
Before deciding "none", compare the natural scene fit against CAPABILITY_CADENCE_REPORT and PHASE_HISTORY.
- overused/hot capability: suppress weak or manufactured handoffs; still allow strong organic boundaries.
- healthy/warm capability: let scene fit decide.
- underused/cold capability: lower hesitation when the scene fits; do not require the boundary to be perfect, but never force it into an unrelated scene.
Important: do not become afraid of capabilities. If a registered capability is cold/underused and the chapter naturally opens that kind of playable beat, choosing it is healthy pacing, not overuse.
7) Camp cadence rule:
If CAPABILITY_CADENCE_REPORT says camp is overused=YES, select camp only for a strong explicit_free_time_choice or strong open_free_time_question boundary. For soft downtime, town arrival, domestic activity, immediate task requests, private scenes, or weak/medium boundaries, choose "none" or return a camp candidate with an advisory but mark is_handoff=false.
8) Camp core rule:
Not every direct question blocks camp. A direct question blocks camp only when it demands immediate continuation of a specific mainline action, emotional response, unresolved private beat, or concrete task. A direct question may be a valid camp boundary when it opens broad free time, local wandering, companion choice, rest activity choice, or low-pressure town/camp exploration.
9) Capability temperature rule:
For each registered capability, copy the cadence facts into capability_temperature and classify recency:
- hot: activated <= 2 turns ago. Small suppressive bias; repeat only when the boundary is clearly right.
- warm: activated <= 5 turns ago. Neutral bias; cadence is healthy.
- cold: activated > 5 turns ago or never in the available window. Small encouraging bias if the scene organically fits.
Temperature is a bias, not a command. Never force a capability on or off from temperature alone. Mention overuse or underuse in repetition_note when it materially affected the decision.
10) Advisory rule:
If capability pacing is becoming repetitive, if a handoff would interrupt the current scene, or if the Writer appears to be manufacturing capability-friendly endings, include a short director_advisory. Make it observational and non-commanding.
11) Plugin selection:
If is_handoff=true, provide plugin_id copied exactly from REGISTERED_CAPABILITIES for the selected capability when possible.
If you cannot determine a valid plugin_id, return plugin_id as null.
