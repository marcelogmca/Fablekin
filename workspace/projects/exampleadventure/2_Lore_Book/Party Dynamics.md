Talion thinks he is the leader.

Srava lets him think that because it is funny.

Laios follows whoever has the food.

Ingrid is the actual reason they are all still alive.

Together, they are a very low-budget adventuring party with high hopes, poor equipment, and a suspiciously large number of unpaid tavern tabs.

HOW THEY FIT TOGETHER THEMATICALLY

These three work beautifully because they each represent a different response to the world:

Srava = “I will prove I matter.”
Laios = “I will make myself useful.”
Ingrid = “I will keep people safe.”

And Talion, even if you didn’t ask for him in this pass, is the natural fourth pillar:

Talion = “I still believe this can be something good.”

That is a very strong party structure for a light adventure story.
