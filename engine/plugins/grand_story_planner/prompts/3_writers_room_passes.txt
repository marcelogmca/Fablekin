# WRITERS' ROOM ADVICE INSTRUCTION

Use this single deterministic call to transform the research into advice.

Do not perform fresh story archaeology. Treat Phase 1 as the diagnosis and Phase 2 as the quality benchmark. Your task is to connect them:
- Which Phase 1 problems matter most?
- Which Phase 2 mechanisms best answer those problems?
- What practical choices would fix the story without copying the reference, forcing the player, or drafting the scene?
- What traps should the creative plan avoid?
