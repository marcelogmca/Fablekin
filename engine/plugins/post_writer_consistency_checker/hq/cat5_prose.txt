## Rules

**Visual-engine-aware leanness.** Cut environmental description the renderer already shows. Redundant description stalls the scene to restate what is already on screen - focus on character action, micro-expression, and dialogue instead.
- Bad: Three paragraphs describing the shape of the chairs, the grain of the table, the exact blue of the curtains - all visible on screen.
- Bad: A full page cataloguing armor trims, banner folds, and floor tiles while the argument stalls.
- Bad: Re-describing the tavern exterior in detail every time the party walks in.

**One invisible sensory anchor.** Ground each scene with at most one engine-invisible detail (sound, smell, temperature). Piling on senses does not deepen immersion - they blur together and none of them register.
- Bad: A pile-up - cold air, sizzling fat, damp wool, pine smoke, distant bells - all in one beat.
- Bad: Every paragraph introduces a new dominant smell, then a new temperature shift, then a new background sound.
- Bad: Listing the taste, texture, and temperature of six dishes plus the room's humidity in a single meal beat.

**Definitive action.** Write physical actions so clearly the state extractors can parse them. Vague movement forces the downstream systems (and the reader) to guess what actually happened.
- Bad: "He sort of moved toward the door with the thing."
- Bad: "She did something with the supplies that might help later."
- Bad: "They went somewhere else for a while and then things were different."

**Impact over filler.** Prefer high-impact dialogue and visceral action. Beats that resolve through convenience read as the world obeying the player rather than operating on its own logic, which collapses stakes.
- Bad: The guard instantly believes an obvious lie, hands over the keys, and wishes them luck - because the plot needs it.
- Bad: The locked vault swings open because someone "just happens" to have the exact rare key with no setup.
- Bad: A hardened enemy surrenders mid-duel because the player gives a single rousing sentence, with no prior doubt or cost.
